How We Work

From complex science to a meaningful experience.

Experience Discovery helps research teams identify what people could explore, hear, manipulate, inhabit, or connect with inside their science and determine which direction is most worth pursuing.

01

Find the opportunity

Clarify the scientific idea, audience, goals, constraints, and the parts of the work that hold the greatest experiential potential.

02

Shape the direction

Explore possible forms through interaction, narrative, visualization, sonification, or other media without assuming a technology in advance.

03

Make it tangible

Create a focused prototype, develop the experience, or prepare a clear direction that can be carried forward to a larger engagement.

About

Science deserves
a creative partner.

Proper Motion was founded by Charlene Atlas, a creative technologist and experience designer whose work spans XR, interactive systems, and emerging technology.

After partnering with researchers and scientists at Microsoft and Meta — helping transform emerging technologies into experiences that could be explored, understood, and evaluated — Charlene founded Proper Motion to explore a bigger question:

What if scientific discovery could be experienced instead of merely explained?

Move people to understand. Move people to remember. Move people to feel.
